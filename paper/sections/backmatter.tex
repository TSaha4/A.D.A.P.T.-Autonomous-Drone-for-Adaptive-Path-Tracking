\section*{Data and Code Availability}
The implementation is hosted at \url{https://github.com/TSaha4/A.D.A.P.T.-Autonomous-Drone-for-Adaptive-Path-Tracking}. Evaluation scripts, recorded results, WPL files, trained weights and a checksummed source-data manifest accompany the manuscript. The three demonstration maps are third-party graphics without flood ground truth or georeferencing; redistribution rights must be confirmed before publication. The Sen1Floods11 labels and imagery and the Copernicus EMS flood delineations are publicly available from their cited sources. SITL telemetry is included with the evaluation results; no physical flight log is available.
