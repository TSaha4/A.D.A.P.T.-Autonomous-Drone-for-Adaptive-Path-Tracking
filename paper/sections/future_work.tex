\section{Future Work}
\label{sec:future}
The immediate engineering priorities are to reject disconnected route legs, reset the servo between deliveries, and verify an explicit reload sequence in ArduPilot and PX4 SITL. A flight-log-calibrated battery model and site-safety assessment are needed before field trials. The optional GeoTIFF conversion should be extended to verified image registration and ground-control points, while direct annotation accuracy needs truth labels on the three demonstration maps. Flood progression should be tested against more independent observed events and terrain-aware models. Finally, allocation should optimise routed fleet completion time while enforcing endurance, payload, and temporal or vertical vehicle separation.
